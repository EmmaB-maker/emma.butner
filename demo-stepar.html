📖 About the Real Study

Images: The images you saw are highly simplified emojis. In the real study, we use validated databases to create image sets averaged on valence. Half the participants see a highly arousing image set, the other half see a low-arousing set. Over the course of the study, participants view multiple unique image sets to maintain their arousal state.

Trials: Participants complete 128 trials total, broken into blocks so we can re-up their arousal level between each block.

✨ Fun fact: I coded this project using PsychoPy!
